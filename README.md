# Business Intelligence & Sales Performance Analytics

## Overview
A business intelligence portfolio project focused on analyzing sales, cost, profitability, supplier performance, customer activity, product performance, and geographic trends using Tableau and Power BI.

The analysis uses a relational business dataset connecting Orders, Customers, Products, Suppliers, and Countries to transform transactional data into interactive dashboards and management-focused insights.

## Business Questions
The project was designed to answer questions such as:

- Which products and suppliers contribute the most to revenue and profitability?
- Which countries and geographic areas generate the strongest business performance?
- How do revenue, cost, and net revenue change over time?
- Which products or suppliers are underperforming?
- How can managers dynamically explore financial performance across products, customers, suppliers, and regions?

## Tools
- Tableau
- Power BI
- Power Query
- Data Modeling
- Calculated Measures
- Business Intelligence
- Data Visualization

## Data Model
The analysis connects five core business datasets:

- Orders
- Customers
- Products
- Suppliers
- Countries

Key financial measures include:

- **Gross Revenue** = Quantity × List Price
- **Cost** = Quantity × Unit Cost
- **Net Revenue** = Gross Revenue − Cost

## Tableau Analysis
Tableau was used to build interactive analyses including:

- Revenue, cost, and net revenue by geography
- Country and supplier drill-down analysis
- Supplier contribution to net revenue
- Product performance comparisons
- Monthly and daily financial trends
- Parameter-driven financial measure selection
- Geographic maps
- Top and bottom product analysis
- Level of Detail (LOD) calculations
- Interactive filters, sets, and dashboard controls

## Power BI Analysis
Power BI was used to:

- Build a relational data model across the five business datasets
- Transform and clean source data with Power Query
- Analyze product, supplier, customer, and geographic performance
- Compare revenue, cost, and net revenue over time
- Create dynamic financial views and interactive reporting experiences

## Business Value
The dashboards support management decisions by helping users:

- Identify revenue-driving products and suppliers
- Detect underperforming business segments
- Compare profitability across regions
- Monitor financial performance over time
- Explore business results interactively through filters and drill-downs
